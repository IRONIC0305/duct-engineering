ABOUT US

A multidisciplinary consultancy, headquartered in Dhaka since 2009.

Company profile

DUCT Engineering began as a proprietary firm in 2009 and was incorporated as a Private Limited Company in 2013. We provide integrated consultancy across the whole project cycle, from feasibility assessment and detailed design through construction supervision and project management to post-implementation evaluation.

Our core team of about 50 professionals includes engineers, architects, planners, environmental and social specialists, economists and surveyors. We work from our own six-storey headquarters in Dhaka.

Our expertise covers transportation, bridges, water resources, flood control and drainage, buildings, urban infrastructure, geotechnical engineering, environmental management, electrical and mechanical engineering, and social infrastructure.

OUR VISION

To be a leading multidisciplinary engineering consultancy recognized nationally and internationally for delivering innovative, sustainable and high-quality infrastructure solutions that contribute to economic growth, environmental stewardship and social well-being.

OUR MISSION

To provide integrated, innovative and sustainable engineering consultancy services that deliver practical and value-driven solutions for infrastructure development, supporting governments, development partners and private clients through technical excellence, professional integrity and client-focused service.
